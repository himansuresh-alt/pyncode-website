🥕 What is Pyncode Chops?

A simple local business concept focused on chopped and ready-to-cook vegetables.

📍 Every Pincode → Local Pyncode Chops Partner
Pyncode Chops is planned as a Pincode-focused chopped vegetable business model.

A local partner can serve customers in their selected service area with fresh-cut and ready-to-cook vegetable products.
